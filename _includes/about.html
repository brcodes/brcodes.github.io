Bio

Bryce is a computational biological scientist and engineer with nearly a half decade of experience in bioinspired graduate-level modeling, analysis, visualization and development (Ph.D Bioengineering OSU, Python & Git ecosystems). Adding to a previous quarter of a decade in protein engineering at the benchtop (M.S. Chemistry CSU), he now brings a mix of quantitative science experience in both strictly computational and live biochemical/chemical systems. His aim is to create, implement or augment informed systems for the betterment of biological, chemical or adjacent technology and study.

Personally, his love for engineering has expressed itself through AI-assisted web app development. He also enjoys composing music, and dedicates all of his work to the glory of God.
